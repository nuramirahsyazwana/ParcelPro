Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how information is handled when you use the ParcelPro website and its demonstration services.

Prototype notice: This sample privacy policy describes the current student-project demonstration. It is not a substitute for a legally reviewed policy for a live business.

01 Information entered

Forms may ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, delivery details or rider interest information. In this prototype, some account and parcel data is stored in your browser using localStorage.

02 How information is used

Information is used only to demonstrate account screens, parcel tracking, shipping estimates and sample registration interactions.

03 Storage and security

Data stored in localStorage remains in the browser profile and may be accessible to people using the same device or browser profile. This prototype does not provide production-grade security, encryption or server-side access control.

04 External services

The site may load interface libraries and fonts from third-party CDNs and link to social media or video platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

05 Your choices

You can clear this prototype's locally stored website data through your browser settings. Do not submit sensitive personal or payment information.

06 Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@parcelpro.example .
