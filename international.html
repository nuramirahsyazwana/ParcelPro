Shipping Requirements

Information needed before submitting an international parcel.

  • Provide the recipient's full name and valid contact number.
  • Enter a complete delivery address, including postal code and country.
  • Provide the correct parcel weight and package type.
  • Ensure that the parcel is securely packed before shipping.
  • Declare the parcel contents accurately when required.
  • Additional customs duties, taxes or charges may apply depending on the destination country.

Important Shipping Rules

Please review these rules before sending your parcel.

  • Prohibited and restricted items must not be included in parcels.
  • Dangerous goods, illegal substances and other restricted materials are not accepted.
  • Some products may require permits or additional documentation.
  • Customers are responsible for ensuring that their items comply with destination-country regulations.
  • Delivery times may vary due to customs inspections, weather conditions and operational delays.
Important Notice: The customer should check the import regulations of the destination country before submitting an international shipment. ParcelPro may request additional information or reject shipments that do not meet the applicable requirements.

International Shipping Fees

Understand how your estimated international shipping fee is calculated.

International shipping fees are estimated based on the destination country, parcel weight and package type. The estimated fee will be displayed when you complete the shipping form in your customer account.

The administrator may review and confirm the final shipping fee after checking the shipment details.
